# Motion Designer's playbook — transitions, camera, FX, look

## Transition vocabulary (with meaning; ≥ 70% plain cuts in talking heads)
| transition_in | use for | auto SFX |
|---|---|---|
| `cut` | default | – |
| `punch` | new point, emphasis | pop |
| `whip_left/right` | change place/topic, "next" | whoosh |
| `whip_up/down` | list step, reveal | whoosh |
| `zoom_blur` | going into a detail/result | whoosh |
| `flash` | before/after, reveal, beat drop | flash |
| `glitch` | wrong vs right, tech, surprise | glitch |
Never two flashy transitions back-to-back in talking heads. In montages, whips/flashes on strong beats are the style.

## Camera motion
- `zoom_from/zoom_to` slow push (1.00→1.06) or pull (1.08→1.00) on every shot: footage should never feel static.
- `shake` 0.3–0.6 on impacts/punchlines/engine revs, one segment at a time.
- `zoom_punches` for emphasis (coordinate with the Cutter).

## FX (`fx`)
- `letterbox` 0.08–0.12 (cinematic, luxury). `vhs` 0.6–1.0 (retro). `chroma` 0.15–0.35 (tech, hype).
- `beat_flash` 0.15–0.3 with `beats_out` (montage on music; autoplan fills it).

## Stickers
Emoji or PNG (logo, screenshot). Pop + float + wobble automatic. 1.2–1.6 s each, ≤ 1 per 4–6 s,
at x 0.2/0.8 away from the face/product. A logo PNG on the final 2–3 s for brands.

## Colour look (`grade`)
punchy (default social) · cinematic (teal-orange) · clean_bright (skin, food, indoor) · warm_film (+grain 5–7)
· moody (cars at night, serious) · bw_contrast · none. Check skin on preview frames: orange skin → clean_bright.
Cars: moody or cinematic; keep paint colour true (compare a frame with the source).
