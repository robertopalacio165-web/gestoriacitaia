# Reels & Shorts in 2026 — what works (Director's playbook)

## Retention structure (30 s reel)
| Time | Beat | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| 0–1.5 s | **Hook**: strongest sentence + hook text box + motion | stop the scroll |
| 1.5–5 s | **Context / promise**: "here's how / why / what happened" | earn the next 25 s |
| 5–24 s | **Value**: 3 fast points or story beats, a pattern interrupt every 2–3 s | retention |
| 24–28 s | **Payoff / proof**: result, before/after, punchline | satisfaction, shares |
| 28–30 s | **CTA or loop**: follow/comment keyword, or end on a line that flows into the first frame | rewatches, comments |

Hook formulas: bold claim ("ما كاينش لي كيقولها ليك..."), number ("3 حوايج..."), negative ("حبس دير هادي"),
result-first (show the end result, then "هاكيفاش"), question to the viewer, open loop ("الثالثة هي لي بدلات كلشي").

## 2026 style menu (pick one family, don't mix all)
1. **Talking head, high-energy (Hormozi/edutainment)** — jump cuts with zero air, alternate zoom,
   2–3 word captions with highlight, punch-ins on key words, emoji stickers, SFX on everything, progress bar.
   Presets: captions `hormozi` or `bold_pop`, grade `punchy`, transitions mostly `cut`/`punch`.
2. **Clean creator / lifestyle (aesthetic)** — slower 2.5–4 s beats, `clean_minimal` or `karaoke_fill`
   captions, `warm_film` or `clean_bright` grade + grain, few transitions (`zoom_blur`, `whip`), soft music.
3. **Cinematic story / vlog** — `cinematic` grade, `boxed` labels for places/times, speed ramps
   (segment speed 1.5 into 1.0), whips between locations, riser into the payoff.
4. **Tech / business / tutorial** — `neon_glow` or `bold_pop`, `number` texts for steps (1, 2, 3),
   `label` callouts, screen B-roll via `blurfill`, `glitch` only for "wrong vs right".
5. **Lo-fi authentic** — minimal editing on purpose: hard cuts, `clean_minimal` captions, `none` grade.
   Use when the content is raw/emotional; over-editing kills trust.

## Trends that matter now
- Word-by-word animated captions are expected (≈85% watch muted at first); highlight the spoken word.
- Faster hooks, but **less clutter** than 2023-24: one strong idea per screen, big type, clean safe zones.
- Text-led storytelling (on-screen statements carry the story even without sound).
- Seamless loops (last frame/line connects to the first) — boosts rewatch rate.
- Sound design as a signature: whoosh on movement, pop on text, impact on reveals, riser into payoff.
- Native, authentic faces > stock footage; subtle film grain and warm skin tones.
- Comment-keyword CTAs ("اكتب 'مونطاج' فالكومنطير") outperform "follow for more".
- Series formats ("Part 1/3"), numbered lists, before/after splits.

## Director's checklist
- Watch everything (contact sheets) and read the whole transcript before cutting.
- Write the brief in plan comments or chat: hook line, 3 beats, payoff, CTA, chosen style family.
- Cut to target ±0.5 s. If the raw is long, choose the best 30 s, not the first 30 s.
- Hook text ≤ 6 words, says the promise, not the topic.
